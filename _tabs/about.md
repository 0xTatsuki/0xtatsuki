Hey i'm Tatsuki, a french Security Analyst with a background in Vulnerability Management and Identity & Access Management (IAM), currently on a journey into the offensive security world.

After spending time on the defensive side, i decided to shift my focus toward penetration testing and red team operations. It's a challenging path, but one that perfectly fits my mindset : i'm naturally curious, always eager to learn and i genuinely enjoy throwing myself into new challenges.

This blog is where i document my journey through :

- Machines writeups from HackTheBox and other platforms
- Challenges solutions from events i've participated in (CyberApocalypse, 404CTF, ...) 
- Certification reviews - my exam experiences, tips and more on certs like eJPT, CPTS, ...
- Tech exploration - experimenting with new tools, techniques and technologies

Whether you're on a similar path or just passing through, i hope you find something useful here.
